LankaGlyphset ⇄ Unicode Converter

Convert LankaGlyphset glyph names (e.g. kRa-sinh) to their Sinhala Unicode string and back. Paste entries on one line or many, separated by spaces, commas, or nothing at all. Enumerated glyphs show their glyphset tier kernel; well-formed names not enumerated in the glyphset are marked derived.
